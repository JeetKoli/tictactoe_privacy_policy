Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 • Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1 Overview & Commitment

Thank you for playing Tic Tac Toe. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data and third-party advertising services.

Summary: We do not require accounts or collect your personal contact information directly. Game scores and preferences stay on your device, and we use Google AdMob to display Banner ads.

2 Personal Information

When you play Tic Tac Toe:

  • We do not require registration, accounts, or logins.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or physical address.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

3 Local Data Storage (Game Stats & Settings)

The game saves your win/draw statistics, game mode, AI difficulty, sound toggle, and light/dark theme preference locally on your device using Android DataStore / local storage.

This gameplay data stays within the app's sandboxed storage on your device and can be cleared anytime by tapping "Reset Score" inside the app or clearing app data in Android settings.

4 Permissions & System Hardware

Tic Tac Toe adheres to the principle of least privilege:

  • No Dangerous Permissions: The app does not request dangerous runtime permissions (such as Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Storage).
  • Network Access & Connectivity: An active internet connection is required to play (gameplay pauses automatically if internet is turned off). Standard internet and network state permissions are used to verify connectivity and load Banner ads via Google AdMob.

5 Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Tic Tac Toe free to play, this app displays Banner advertisements served by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain technical data—such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device information, and ad interaction metrics—to deliver, personalize, and measure ads.

You can manage or opt out of personalized ads anytime in Android Settings > Google > Ads. For more details, please review Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

6 Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Parents and guardians can manage advertising personalization settings on their device through Android's Privacy and Ads controls.

7 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document and updated inside the app.
